# Thematic verb endings

A thematic verb puts a thematic vowel between its tense stem and the personal ending. The vowel is ο before μ or ν and ε elsewhere (Smyth §377–378, §456). The tenses come in two groups (Smyth §360): the primary tenses (present, future, perfect) and the secondary tenses (imperfect, aorist), which take the [augment](augment) and different endings (Smyth §462–464).
