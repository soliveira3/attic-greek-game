# The augment

The augment marks past time. It appears only in the past tenses of the indicative: the imperfect, the aorist and the pluperfect (Smyth §428). Infinitives never have it: see [infinitives](infinitives).

## The syllabic augment
A verb that begins with a consonant puts ε in front, which adds a syllable (Smyth §429): {{form verb:λύω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg aor.act.ind.1.sg}}.

## The temporal augment
A verb that begins with a vowel lengthens it instead (Smyth §435): α and ε become η, ο becomes ω, and a diphthong lengthens its first vowel, so αι becomes ῃ.
- {{form verb:ἄγω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἐθέλω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ὀνομάζω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἀκούω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:αἰσθάνομαι pres.mp.ind.1.sg impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}

A vowel that is already long stays as it is (Smyth §436), so the augment cannot be seen: {{form verb:ἥκω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}. Some verbs beginning with a diphthong may leave it unaugmented (Smyth §437): {{form verb:εὑρίσκω aor.act.ind.1.sg}}.

## Compound verbs
In a verb compounded with a preposition, the augment stands between the preposition and the verb (Smyth §449). The preposition loses its final vowel before the augment, and a preposition that changed its form in the present gets its own form back:
- {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:καταλαμβάνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:συμβαίνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}

## In the game
A missing, wrong or misplaced augment counts as an augment error, and so does the present typed for the imperfect, or the imperfect for the present.
