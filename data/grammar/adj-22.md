# Two-ending adjectives

Some adjectives of the 2nd declension have no separate feminine: the masculine forms serve for both genders, so they have only two sets of endings (Smyth §288). Most of them are compounds. {{word adj:ἄδικος}} is made of the negative prefix ἀ- and the root of {{word noun:δίκη}} (Smyth §289).

{{table adj:ἄδικος}}

## What to notice
- The masculine and feminine are always alike: {{form adj:ἄδικος nom.sg.m nom.sg.f}}.
- The neuter is declined like {{word noun:δῶρον}}: {{form adj:ἄδικος nom.sg.n nom.pl.n}}.
- The masculine and feminine vocative singular ends in -ε, as in {{word noun:λόγος}}: {{form adj:ἄδικος voc.sg.m}}.
