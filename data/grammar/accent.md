# Accents and breathings

## Breathings
Every word that begins with a vowel or a diphthong has a breathing on it: rough, sounded as h, or smooth, not sounded (Smyth §9). An initial υ always has the rough breathing (Smyth §10), and so does an initial ρ (Smyth §13). On a diphthong the breathing stands on the second vowel (Smyth §11): {{form verb:αἰσθάνομαι pres.mp.ind.1.sg}}.

## The three accents
- The acute can stand on any of the last three syllables (Smyth §149).
- The circumflex stands only on a long vowel or a diphthong, and only on one of the last two syllables (Smyth §149).
- The grave replaces an acute on the last syllable when another word follows (Smyth §154). The game shows words on their own, so you will always see and type the acute there.

When the last syllable is long, the accent cannot stand three syllables from the end (Smyth §163), and an accented long next-to-last syllable takes the circumflex only when the last syllable is short (Smyth §164). Final -αι and -οι count as short for the accent (Smyth §169).

## Nouns and adjectives: persistent accent
A noun or adjective keeps its accent on the syllable it has in the nominative singular, as far as the last syllable allows (Smyth §205): {{form noun:θάλαττα nom.sg gen.sg nom.pl}}. Words of the 1st and 2nd declensions accented on the last syllable take a circumflex in the genitive and dative (Smyth §207): {{form noun:τιμή nom.sg gen.sg dat.sg}}. The genitive plural of the 1st declension always has the circumflex on its last syllable (Smyth §208): {{form noun:χώρα gen.pl}}.

## Verbs: recessive accent
A finite verb puts its accent as far from the end as the last syllable allows (Smyth §423): {{form verb:λύω pres.act.ind.1.sg pres.act.ind.1.pl impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}. Infinitives are an exception: see [infinitives](infinitives).

## In the game
With the default setting, ignore accents, a wrong accent is pointed out but the answer still counts. Strict mode counts accents; lenient mode ignores breathings and the iota subscript too. Breathings count in every mode except lenient.
