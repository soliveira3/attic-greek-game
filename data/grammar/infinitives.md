# Infinitives

The infinitive is a noun made from a verb (Smyth §358). It has tense and voice but no person or number, and it never takes the [augment](augment), which belongs to the indicative (Smyth §428), not even in the aorist. Compare {{form verb:λύω aor.act.ind.1.sg}} with {{form verb:λύω aor.act.inf}}, and {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω aor.act.ind.1.sg}} with {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω aor.act.inf}}.

## Endings
Each infinitive is built on its tense stem (Smyth §469):
- In the present, the future active and the 2nd aorist active, the ending merges with the thematic vowel: {{form verb:λύω pres.act.inf fut.act.inf}}, {{form verb:λείπω aor.act.inf}}. A contracted future has a contracted infinitive: {{form verb:μένω fut.act.inf}}.
- The 1st aorist active ends in -αι: {{form verb:λύω aor.act.inf}}.
- The aorist passive and the perfect active end in -αι after ν: {{form verb:λύω aor.pass.inf perf.act.inf}}.
- The middle and middle/passive infinitives, and the future passive, end in -αι after σθ: {{form verb:λύω pres.mp.inf fut.mid.inf aor.mid.inf perf.mp.inf fut.pass.inf}}.

## Accent
The accent of a finite verb follows a rule of its own (see [accents and breathings](accent)); the infinitive does not regularly obey it (Smyth §425). Many infinitives happen to be accented as far back as the finite rule would put them, but several types have a fixed place for the accent instead:
- on the syllable before the last, in infinitives ending in -αι after ν, in the 1st aorist active, the 2nd aorist middle and the perfect middle: {{form verb:λύω aor.pass.inf perf.act.inf aor.act.inf perf.mp.inf}}, {{form verb:λείπω aor.mid.inf}};
- on the last syllable, with a circumflex, in the 2nd aorist active and in contracted futures: there the accent was on the vowel that then merged with the ending (Smyth §424, §469): {{form verb:λείπω aor.act.inf}}, {{form verb:μένω fut.act.inf}}.
