# Infinitives

The infinitive is a verbal noun: it has tense and voice but no person, and it never takes the [augment](augment), even in the aorist. Compare {{form verb:λύω aor.act.ind.1.sg}} with {{form verb:λύω aor.act.inf}}, and {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω aor.act.ind.1.sg}} with {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω aor.act.inf}}.

## Endings
The endings are added to the tense stem (Smyth §469):
- The present, the future active and the 2nd aorist active contract the thematic vowel with the ending: {{form verb:λύω pres.act.inf fut.act.inf}}, {{form verb:λείπω aor.act.inf}}.
- The 1st aorist active ends in -αι: {{form verb:λύω aor.act.inf}}.
- The aorist passive and the perfect active end in -αι after ν: {{form verb:λύω aor.pass.inf perf.act.inf}}.
- The middle and middle/passive infinitives end in -αι after σθ: {{form verb:λύω pres.mp.inf fut.mid.inf aor.mid.inf perf.mp.inf}}. So does the future passive: {{form verb:λύω fut.pass.inf}}.

## Accent
Infinitives do not follow the recessive accent of the finite verb (Smyth §425). Those ending in -αι after ν, the 1st aorist active, the 2nd aorist middle and the perfect middle accent the next-to-last syllable: {{form verb:λύω aor.pass.inf aor.act.inf perf.mp.inf}}, {{form verb:λείπω aor.mid.inf}}. The 2nd aorist active accents its ending: {{form verb:λείπω aor.act.inf}}.
