# Adjectives of the 3rd declension

Adjectives that belong only to the 3rd declension have two endings: one set of forms for the masculine and feminine, one for the neuter (Smyth §291). Most of them have stems in ες, with nominatives in -ης and -ες, or in ον, with nominatives in -ων and -ον.

## Stems in ες
{{word adj:ἀληθής}} is declined like the σ-stem nouns: the σ was lost between vowels and the vowels contracted (Smyth §292, §263). See [σ-stems](decl3-s).

## Stems in ον
{{word adj:εὐδαίμων}} is declined like the nasal stem {{word noun:ἡγεμών}}, with the endings -ος, -ι, -α in the singular and -ες, -ων, -σι, -ας in the plural (Smyth §293). See [consonant stems](decl3-consonant).

{{table adj:εὐδαίμων}}

## What to notice
- The neuter nominative, accusative and vocative singular is the bare stem: {{form adj:ἀληθής nom.sg.n}}, {{form adj:εὐδαίμων nom.sg.n}}.
- In the neuter plural these cases end in -α, or in -η where -εα has contracted: {{form adj:εὐδαίμων nom.pl.n}}, {{form adj:ἀληθής nom.pl.n}}.
