# 3rd declension: ευ-stems

Nouns like {{word noun:βασιλεύς}} have a stem in ευ. Before an ending that begins with a vowel the υ was lost (Smyth §278), so most cases show only ε before the ending.

{{table noun:βασιλεύς}}

## What to notice
- The genitive singular ends in -ως and the accusative singular in a long α (Smyth §275): {{form noun:βασιλεύς gen.sg acc.sg}}.
- The vocative singular is the only form that shows the bare stem (Smyth §278): {{form noun:βασιλεύς voc.sg}}.
- Before a consonant the υ stays, as in the nominative and the dative plural: {{form noun:βασιλεύς nom.sg dat.pl}}.
- The nominative plural is contracted and takes a circumflex: {{form noun:βασιλεύς nom.pl}}.
