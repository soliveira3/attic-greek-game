# The augment

When an indicative verb refers to the past, it carries an extra element at its front: the augment. You will find it on the imperfect, the aorist and the pluperfect, and nowhere else (Smyth §428): other moods and the [infinitives](infinitives) never have it.

## The syllabic augment
A verb whose first sound is a consonant adds an extra syllable, ε, in front (Smyth §429): {{form verb:λύω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg aor.act.ind.1.sg}}.

## The temporal augment
A verb whose first sound is a vowel makes it long instead (Smyth §435): α and ε become η, ο becomes ω, and in a diphthong the first vowel lengthens, so αι becomes ῃ.
- {{form verb:ἄγω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἐθέλω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ὀνομάζω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἀκούω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:αἰσθάνομαι pres.mp.ind.1.sg impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}

A vowel that is long already cannot be lengthened (Smyth §436), so the augment does not show: {{form verb:ἥκω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}. Some verbs that begin with a diphthong may leave it as it is (Smyth §437): {{form verb:εὑρίσκω aor.act.ind.1.sg}}.

## Verbs that once began with a consonant
A few verbs lost a consonant at their start long ago, but still take the syllabic ε, which then merges with the vowel (Smyth §431):
- {{form verb:ἔχω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἕπομαι pres.mp.ind.1.sg impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:ἐργάζομαι pres.mp.ind.1.sg impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}

A few others can take either ε or η (Smyth §430): {{form verb:βούλομαι impf.mp.ind.1.sg}}, {{form verb:μέλλω impf.act.ind.1.sg}}.

## Compound verbs
When a verb is compounded with a preposition, the augment goes after the preposition, right before the verb itself (Smyth §449). The preposition drops its final vowel, and one that had changed its shape in the present returns to its own form:
- {{form verb:ἀποκτείνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:καταλαμβάνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}
- {{form verb:συμβαίνω pres.act.ind.1.sg impf.act.ind.1.sg}}

## In the game
A missing, wrong or misplaced augment is marked as an augment error, and so is the present typed for the imperfect, or the imperfect for the present. Where the augment cannot be seen, as with {{word verb:ἥκω}}, the same slip counts as an ending error.
